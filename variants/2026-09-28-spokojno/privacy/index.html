Privacy Policy

Fill in the Blank Generator · Last updated 28 September 2026

Fill in the Blank Generator is a Chrome extension that turns a text into a worksheet with gaps and an answer key. There is no account and no sign-up, it shows no ads, and we never sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • The text you highlight on a page, paste or type, and the worksheets you make from it. A sheet in progress is kept in your browser for the tab you are working in and is gone when you close the browser.
  • Your settings: the look of the sheet, the export format and the last choices in the Generate form. They are saved in your browser’s own extension storage.

What is sent to our server, and why

Only when you use one of these features, and only what the feature needs:

  • Photo and Screenshot the page: the image, so the text on it can be read and returned to your panel.
  • Use your phone: the photo you take on your phone. The text read from it waits on the server for up to 10 minutes until your panel picks it up, and is deleted as soon as it is picked up.
  • Generate: what you ask for — the grammar point or words, level, age group, kind of text, topic and length — so a text can be written.
  • Choice and Word formation exercises: the sentences that hold the gaps, so answer options and word roots can be found.
Our server is a small program on Cloudflare. It passes these requests to the AI provider Anthropic (Claude), which processes them to return the result. Our server does not keep your images, texts or requests, except the 10-minute wait for a phone photo described above.

Fair-use limit and counts

  • The extension creates a random install number. It is sent with the requests above and is used only for a daily fair-use limit. The daily count for that number is kept for up to 2 days.
  • The server keeps daily totals of requests — numbers only, without any content — to see how the service is used.
  • Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver a request and to limit how often requests can be made. We do not store it.

What the extension can see

The extension works only on the tab where you click its icon, and only after you click it: it has no permission to read all websites. On that page it reads only what you highlight, or the whole page when you click Whole page, or a picture of what you see when you click Screenshot the page. It does not collect your browsing history, and there is no analytics or tracking in the extension.

Permissions, and what each is for:

  • activeTab and scripting — to work on the tab where you clicked the icon: the marker, Whole page and Screenshot the page.
  • sidePanel — to open the extension next to the page.
  • storage — to keep your sheet in progress and your settings in your browser.
  • Optional access to newsinlevels.com, learningenglish.voanews.com and simple.wikipedia.org — asked only when you first open one of them with Find an article, so the marker can start there by itself.
